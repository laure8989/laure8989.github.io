Case Study — Campaign Development

Welcome to Wonder

Client
Hocking Hills
Industry
Travel & Tourism
Role
Senior Designer
Timeline
2026

The Challenge

With “Welcome to Wonder” established as the campaign platform, the challenge was giving the idea a visual identity of its own. The creative needed to capture the scale and beauty of Hocking Hills while extending beyond the trails to dining, attractions, and experiences in Logan. Most importantly, it needed to feel distinct while still building naturally from the destination’s existing brand.

The Insight

The landscape didn’t need more decoration. It needed room to make an impact. By pairing immersive photography with bold typography, an expanded earth-inspired palette, and playful compositions, the campaign could let the natural wonder lead while creating a flexible visual language for everything beyond it.

The Solution

I brought “Welcome to Wonder” to life through a flexible campaign system built around bold typography, immersive photography, and a nature-inspired color palette. Layered compositions balance the scale of the landscape with playful moments of personality, allowing the creative to shift seamlessly from outdoor adventure to dining, family attractions, and experiences in Logan. The system was then carried across print, digital, OOH, and environmental applications to create a cohesive campaign without making every execution feel the same.
