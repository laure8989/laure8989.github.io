Case Study 01 — Branding & Web

Sherrie's Wheatons

Sherrie’s Wheatons needed a brand identity and website that reflected the care, expertise, and personality behind her breeding program. I created a cohesive visual identity and digital experience that helped showcase her kennel, educate prospective owners, and build trust around the Soft Coated Wheaten Terrier breed.

The problem

Sherrie’s kennel had no recognizable brand or online presence, making it difficult for prospective owners to discover and trust her program.

Without a recognizable brand or website, prospective puppy buyers struggled to discover Sherrie's kennel, evaluate her credibility, or learn about responsible breeding practices. This limited her visibility and made it harder to connect with qualified buyers while advocating for the preservation of the breed.

Background & goals

Sherrie is a Soft Coated Wheaton Terrier breeder with little to no online or visual presence. She wanted to establish a brand and website that reflected her kennel's personality while educating prospective buyers about the dwindling breed.

  1. 01Create a trustworthy brand and website that helped prospective puppy buyers confidently discover and connect with Sherrie's kennel.
  2. 02Design a site that was simple for first-time puppy buyers to navigate while communicating Sherrie's expertise and values.
  3. 03A cohesive brand and website that increased credibility, educated prospective buyers, and gave Sherrie a lasting online presence to support future litters.

Research → strategy

Research focused on understanding Sherrie’s breeding philosophy, the Soft Coated Wheaten Terrier breed, and the visual landscape of existing breeder brands. I analyzed competitor identities, breeder websites, and breed organizations to uncover opportunities to create a more memorable and trustworthy presence. The research revealed that many breeder brands lacked personality and relied on generic visuals, creating an opportunity for Sherrie’s kennel to stand out through a warm, authentic identity rooted in care, expertise, and breed preservation. These insights guided the development of a brand system and website experience that reflected the kennel’s personality while building trust with prospective owners.
